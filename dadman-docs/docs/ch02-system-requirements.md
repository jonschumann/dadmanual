Download this version from your Avid Master Account, not from the DAD support portal. Using an unapproved version may cause compatibility issues with your Avid hardware.

## 2.2 DAD Thunderbolt 3 Driver System Requirements

> **NOTICE:** This section applies only if your hardware is an AX64, AX Center, Core 256, or an Avid MTRX product. If your hardware connects to the computer over Ethernet only (e.g., AX32, DX32R, Penta series), the Thunderbolt 3 driver is not required.

| Requirement | macOS | Windows |
|-------------|-------|---------|
| **Supported OS versions** | macOS 11 Big Sur and later (Intel and Apple Silicon) | Windows 10, Windows 11 |
| **Minimum DADman version** | 5.6.7.1 or later | 5.6.7.1 or later |
| **Thunderbolt cable** | Thunderbolt 3 or USB-C cable rated to 20 Gbps minimum | Thunderbolt 3 or USB-C cable rated to 20 Gbps minimum |
| **Compatible hardware** | AX64, AX Center, Core 256, Avid MTRX, Avid MTRX II, Avid MTRX Studio, Dynaudio Control 01 | AX64, AX Center, Core 256, Avid MTRX, Avid MTRX II, Avid MTRX Studio |

> **CAUTION — Cable specification:** Using a cable that is not rated to at least 20 Gbps may result in unstable or failed Thunderbolt connections. Standard USB-C cables that do not carry Thunderbolt certification are not sufficient. Verify your cable before installation.
