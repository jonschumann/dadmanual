# Chapter 1 — Introduction

> **Product:** Digital Audio Denmark AX Center

---

## 1.1 Product Overview

The AX Center is a 1U rackmount Thunderbolt 3 audio interface and the primary I/O hub in the Thunder|Core family. It provides front-panel mic/instrument inputs, dual headphone outputs, stereo monitor outputs, MADI I/O, ADAT I/O, word clock I/O, two analogue expansion card slots, and dual Thunderbolt 3 ports.

**Connection to host:** Thunderbolt 3
**Form factor:** 1U rackmount
**Audio channels:** Up to 256×256 channels at 48 kHz

---

## 1.2 Key Features

![AX Center — front panel](/img/panel-ax-center-front.png)
*AX Center — front panel*

![AX Center — rear panel](/img/panel-ax-center-rear.png)
*AX Center — rear panel*

- **Form factor:** 1U rackmount, 19"
- **Expansion:** 2 hot-plug expansion card slots
- **Thunderbolt 3:** 2 × USB-C — 256 channels at 48/96 kHz; 15 W bus power per port
- **Dante AoIP:** Built-in — 256 channels at 48 kHz, 128 channels at 96 kHz
- **MADI:** Built-in coaxial MADI I/O — 64 channels at 48 kHz
- **ADAT / S/PDIF:** Built-in ADAT/SMUX (16 ch) and S/PDIF (2 ch)
- **DADlink:** 2 × SFP cages — DADlink (up to 256×256 ch) or MADI optical
- **Native analogue:** 2 × mic/instrument inputs (front panel, combo connector, phantom power); 2 × stereo headphone outputs; 2 × stereo monitor outputs
- **Synchronisation:** Word Clock I/O (BNC, 75 Ω), Video Black Burst input
- **Routing:** 1,006×1,112 matrix; 512×64 summing processor; 1,024-filter SPQ equaliser; 28-bit floating-point
- **Latency:** 7 samples internal delay, adjustable 3–31 samples; all outputs time- and phase-aligned
- **Control:** DADman software (macOS and Windows); Avid EuCon compatible; Gigabit Ethernet
- **Weight / depth:** 3.2 kg / 7.1 lbs; chassis depth 32 cm / 12.6"
