# Chapter 1 — Introduction

> **Product:** Digital Audio Denmark Penta 721s

---

## 1.1 Product Overview

The Penta 721s is the 1U slim variant of the Penta 720, sharing the same modular architecture and Ethernet-only connectivity. It adds an integrated sample rate converter, enabling it to bridge audio streams running at different sample rates within a DADman session.

**Connection to host:** Ethernet (no Thunderbolt)
**Form factor:** 1U rackmount (slim modular chassis)
**Audio channels:** Up to 720 channels with integrated SRC (card-dependent)
