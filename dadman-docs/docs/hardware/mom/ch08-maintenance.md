# Chapter 8 — Maintenance & Firmware

> **Product:** Digital Audio Denmark MOM

---

## 8.1 Firmware Update Procedure

Firmware is updated from within DADman. No separate firmware tool is required.

1. Ensure the unit is connected and visible in the DADman Device List.
2. In DADman, open **Settings → Firmware**.
3. DADman will display available firmware updates. If an update is available for the MOM, it will be listed with the current and available version numbers.
4. Click **Update** and confirm.
5. The unit's front-panel Power button will slow-pulse during the update. **Do not power off or disconnect the unit during the update.**
6. When the update is complete, the unit will restart automatically.
7. Verify the new firmware version in the Device List.

> **CAUTION:** A failed firmware update may leave the unit in an unbootable state. Ensure power and connectivity are stable before initiating a firmware update. Contact DAD support if the unit does not restart after a firmware update.
