## Safety Instructions

### Electrical Safety

**WARNING — Electric shock hazard.** This unit operates from mains power. Do not remove the cover. There are no user-serviceable parts inside. Refer all servicing to qualified service personnel.

- Read all instructions before operating the equipment.
- Keep this manual for future reference.
- Observe all warnings on the equipment and in this manual.
- Do not use this equipment near water.
- Do not block ventilation openings. Install in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions.
- Do not install near heat sources such as radiators or amplifiers.
- Protect the power cord from being walked on or pinched.
- Only use attachments and accessories specified by the manufacturer.
- Unplug this apparatus during lightning storms or when unused for long periods.
- Refer all servicing to qualified service personnel. Servicing is required when the apparatus has been damaged in any way.

### ESD Precautions

> **CAUTION — Electrostatic discharge.** Handle the unit and any expansion cards with care. Electrostatic discharge can damage internal components. Ground yourself before handling internal components or expansion cards.

### Rack Mounting

> **CAUTION — Rack stability.** Ensure the rack or enclosure is stable before installing the unit. Overloading a rack can cause it to tip. Follow the rack manufacturer's instructions for safe mounting and load distribution.

### Thunderbolt Cable
> **NOTICE:** Use only Thunderbolt 3 or Thunderbolt 4 rated cables with this unit. Standard USB-C cables are not rated for Thunderbolt 3 speeds and will not provide reliable operation. Use the cable supplied or a certified replacement.
