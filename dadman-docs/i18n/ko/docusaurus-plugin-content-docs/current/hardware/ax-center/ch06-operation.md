## 6.1 Power On / Off

_[Placeholder — power-on and shutdown procedure via front-panel Power button.]_

### Normal Power-On

1. Ensure all signal cables are connected.
2. Press the front-panel **Power** button.
3. The unit will perform a startup sequence — allow 30 seconds for completion.
4. The **Int.** LED illuminates when the unit is ready.

### Normal Shutdown

1. In DADman, close the session and disconnect the unit.
2. Press the front-panel **Power** button.
3. The button will slow-pulse during the shutdown sequence.
4. Do not remove power while the button is pulsing.

> **CAUTION:** Do not power-cycle the unit during a firmware update. The Power button will slow-pulse during update; wait for the update to complete.
