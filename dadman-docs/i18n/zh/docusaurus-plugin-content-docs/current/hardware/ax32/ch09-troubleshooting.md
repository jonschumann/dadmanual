## 9.4 Contacting Support

If the issue is not resolved by the steps above, contact your local Digital Audio Denmark distributor or DAD support. Provide:

- Unit serial number (rear panel label)
- Firmware version (shown in DADman)
- DADman version
- A description of the symptom and steps already taken
